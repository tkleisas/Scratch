## Full description (max 4000)

Scratch is a vinyl-first two-deck DJ app built for Android.

Load music from your local library and mix on two responsive platters. Scratch, jog, nudge and spin back with low-latency native audio, then shape the mix with an eight-node graphical EQ, independent LP/HP filters, tempo control, key lock and BPM sync.

Build a set with persistent deck queues, queue presets and four-second or custom loops. When it is time to perform, use per-deck and master FX racks with Filter, Crush, Echo, Reverb, Tape, Compressor and Limiter. Each rack has an ordered insert chain and independent punch pads, with Hold and Latch modes.

FEATURES

• Two vinyl platters with scratch, jog and spin-back
• Tempo 50–200%, WSOLA key lock, and deck-to-deck BPM sync
• Eight-node graphical EQ with live pre/post spectra
• Independent low-pass and high-pass filters on each deck
• Loops with IN/OUT points and one-tap LOOP
• Per-rack FX: ordered insert chains plus seven punch pads
• Filter, Crush, Echo, Plate Reverb, Tape, Compressor and Limiter
• Local library search with persistent queues and queue presets
• WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC, OGG/Vorbis and Opus support
• Autoplay, crossfader, channel volume and saved settings
• Landscape interface for phones and tablets

PRIVACY

Scratch is local-first. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, and the app has no network permission. Library metadata, queues, presets, FX state and decoded audio caches stay on the device. Read the privacy policy at https://tkleisas.github.io/scratch/privacy/.

REQUIREMENTS

Android 10 or newer. A local music library is required. Headphones or speakers are recommended for low-latency monitoring.
